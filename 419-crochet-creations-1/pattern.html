Grandma's Sunday Throw

Written by Ana • Inspired by classic living-room afghans

Finished Pattern Photo

This rhythmic, soothing stitch repeat is one of the very first patterns my grandma taught me. It is forgiving enough to work on while listening to kids play in the background, and cozy enough to become a family favorite.

What You Will Need

  • Yarn: 4 skeins of worsted weight (#4) cotton or soft acrylic yarn
  • Hook: 5.5 mm (I-9) crochet hook
  • Notions: Tapestry needle for weaving in ends and scissors
  • Finished Size: Approx. 45" x 55" (easy to adjust in multiples of 3)

Stitch Abbreviations (US Terms)

ch = chain • sc = single crochet • dc = double crochet • sk = skip • st(s) = stitch(es)

Written Instructions

Foundation: Chain 138 (or any multiple of 3 for a custom width).

Row 1: Work 1 sc and 2 dc in the 3rd ch from hook. *Sk 2 ch, work (1 sc, 2 dc) in the next ch.* Repeat from * across until 3 ch remain. Sk 2 ch, 1 sc in the final ch. Turn.

Row 2: Ch 2 (counts as turning chain). Work (1 sc, 2 dc) in the very first sc from the previous row. *Sk the next 2 dc, work (1 sc, 2 dc) in the next sc.* Repeat across, ending with 1 sc in the top of the turning chain. Turn.

Rows 3 – 95: Repeat Row 2 until your throw reaches your favorite cozy length. Fasten off and weave in your ends with a warm cup of tea.
