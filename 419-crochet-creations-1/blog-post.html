The Stories My Grandma Told Between Rows

By Ana • 4 min read

Before picking up my hook for a few quiet morning rows, I always start with a fresh brew. If you love experimenting with your morning cup too, check out this Coffee 101 guide on making the perfect cup.

Journal Story Photo

Whenever I pick up my crochet hook at the end of a long day of motherhood, my mind goes straight back to sitting next to my grandma. She never rushed a single stitch, and she always kept a basket of colorful yarn tucked right beside her chair.

As a kid, I thought she was simply making blankets and scarves. Now that I am raising my own family, I realize she was weaving patience, comfort, and family history into every single row.

"If you drop a stitch, you just gently pull back a few loops and try again. Most things in life are the exact same way."

Finding Quiet in Busy Days

Motherhood is full of noise, laundry piles, and schedules. Having a simple crochet project waiting on the couch gives me ten quiet minutes to breathe, create something tangible with my hands, and remember where my love for yarn began.

Whether you are a seasoned crocheter or learning your very first chain stitch during nap time, I am so glad you are here sharing this space with me.
